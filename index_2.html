Mock exam papers

The bank is split into five papers, each with a closed-book part of about 106 questions and an open-book part of about 48 — the same closed-heavy balance as the real exam. Nothing repeats between papers, and each part keeps its own score and its own clock. Set the minutes per part (0 runs a count-up timer), and tick Done when you are finished. During an exam the 💡 Explain button in the top bar turns instant answers and code references on or off.
